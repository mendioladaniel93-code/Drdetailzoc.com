THE FOUNDER'S STORY

Just a kid from Santa Ana who refused to quit.

Growing up in Santa Ana, I dreamed of becoming a business owner. I didn't have a roadmap or a head start. What I had was the belief that if I kept working, learning, and showing up, I could build something of my own.

When I was working in construction, I used to do the math in my head: three cars in a day, and maybe I could earn what I made on the job. Back then, I didn't fully understand the cost of running a business: equipment, supplies, fuel, insurance, time, payroll, and all the work nobody sees. I learned that revenue isn't profit, and being your own boss doesn't mean taking the easy road.

There were setbacks, mistakes, long days, and moments when the dream felt far away. But I kept going. Every vehicle became a chance to improve my craft. Every customer became a chance to earn trust. Little by little, I started building more than a detailing business.

The goal was never just to work for myself. It was to build something that gives other people a chance to grow.

Today, I'm proud to lead a team. Coaching, teaching, and helping others discover what they're capable of matters just as much to me as the finished result. I want the people around me to develop skills, confidence, and eventually the courage to pursue their own ideas and become entrepreneurs too.

DR DETAILZ represents that journey: starting with what you have, taking pride in your work, and creating something meaningful to share with others. We're here to restore, protect, and maintain your investment—with the same care and commitment that built this company.
